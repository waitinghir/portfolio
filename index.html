About關於我

I've spent 8+ years in product and growth on consumer apps, leading international expansion across Japan, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Southeast Asia. At a community platform, I led the product repositioning for the Japan market, which improved long-term retention by 300%, and built the moderator and creator ecosystem from zero to one — growing posts by 36% and engagement time by 30% through a mix of product and ops incentives.

I also led analytics instrumentation for ML-driven recommendation and search at 20M+ MAU: defining the events and schemas, and working with data engineers and data scientists to validate data quality before it fed experiments and model iterations.

The projects here are where I bring the same habits to building with AI. Nihongo Quiz King's word bank gets the treatment I'd give a data pipeline: a written spec, automated checks, and reviewers that are calibrated before anyone trusts them. The job monitor taught me, again, that a green dashboard isn't a healthy system. Each article is about one judgment call you can take into your own work.

我在消費型 App 做了 8 年以上的產品與成長，負責過日本、台灣、香港和東南亞的海外擴張。在一個社群平台，我主導日本市場的產品重新定位，讓長期留存提升 300%；也從零建立版主與創作者生態，用產品加營運誘因，讓發文數成長 36%、使用時間成長 30%。

我也主導過月活 2,000 萬以上規模的推薦與搜尋埋點：定義事件和資料結構，並和資料工程師、資料科學家一起驗證資料品質，再拿這些資料做實驗和模型迭代。

這裡的專案，是我把同樣的習慣帶進 AI 開發的地方。日文知識王的題庫，我用對待資料管線的方式對待它：先寫規格、再做自動檢查，AI 評審也要先校準過才能信任。求職監控則再一次提醒我：儀表板全綠，不代表系統健康。每篇文章都在講一個判斷，你可以帶回自己的工作裡用。
